About Us

Colebourn Consulting provides independent advice to electricity network and energy sector organisations navigating the shift to a digital, decentralised grid.

With deep experience across network operations, planning and operational technology, we help clients make practical, well-grounded decisions on technology, risk and regulation.

Capabilities

  • Operational technology: ADMS, SCADA and secondary systems strategy and delivery
  • Network planning: asset and network planning for evolving load and generation
  • DER integration: DERMS strategy, dynamic operating envelopes, grid visibility
  • Cyber security: OT security uplift, SOCI Act and AESCSF alignment
  • Regulatory affairs: AER, AEMO and National Electricity Rules obligations
  • Grid transformation: roadmaps and business cases for digital grid programs
